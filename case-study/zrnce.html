Visual Identity · Branding

Zrnce

Zrnce is a visual identity for a coffee-to-go brand, applied across cups, print and social media.

Project information

My role
Brand design
Project type
Visual identity
Applications
Packaging, print, social media

The challenge

The identity needed to be simple and recognisable, flexible enough for cups, posters, billboards and social media, with three flavours that are easy to tell apart.

My approach

I designed the logo, a coffee-bean pattern, the typography and a colour system with one colour per flavour, then applied them consistently across all materials.
