UX/UI Design · Booking Experience

Native Jeeps

A redesign of the Native Jeeps booking experience, covering tour discovery, date and time selection, vehicles and participants, and checkout, on desktop and mobile.

Project information

My role
UX/UI design
Project type
Booking experience
Platform
Web, desktop and mobile
Year
2025

The challenge

Booking a tour involves several decisions in a row: which tour, which date and time, how many vehicles and people, and how to pay. Each step needed to be easy to understand, with the price and the next action always clear.

My approach

I analysed FareHarbor, an established booking system, and adopted the patterns that support decisions: tour cards with photo, price and duration for quick comparison, a tour page that brings reviews, gallery and key details together, and a calendar that reveals available time slots for the selected date. On the tour page, the booking panel stays in view while scrolling, vehicle and participant selection updates the total price, and the checkout drops the main navigation so the final step stays focused.

Studying a proven system before designing helped me separate the patterns that genuinely help people decide from those that only add noise.
