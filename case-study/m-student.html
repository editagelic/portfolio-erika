m-student

m-student brings everyday student services into the Zagrebačka banka mobile app. The showcased screens cover Menza (meal subsidies), IziPay (payments) and m-parking, designed within the bank's existing design system.

The challenge

Each service follows its own logic: Menza revolves around balances and limits, IziPay around choosing an account and a recipient, and m-parking around location, zone and ticket type. The challenge was to bring three different features together into a consistent experience, with a clear overview of key information and an understandable sequence of steps.

My approach

I worked on these flows using the bank's existing components and patterns, together with the product and development team. In Menza, the remaining subsidy, today's spending against the daily limit and the entitlement level sit at the top, with transactions in a separate tab and the QR code one tap away. IziPay keeps the payment in one form, from account to recipient to amount, followed by a confirmation screen that repeats every detail. In m-parking, the vehicle and active ticket stay visible at the top, and ticket types are compared in a single list with zone and price.

Working inside an established design system showed me how much consistency matters: reusing the same inputs, lists and buttons makes each new feature feel familiar from the first use.
